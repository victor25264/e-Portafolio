#### Future Trends in Security and Risk Management

Some of the most important are: 

+ Reputational risk:
  + Advantajes:
    + Environmental management and reporting can help reduce risk
    + Options theory can help consider the information asymmetry
    + Increase importance for companies (shareholder value)
  + Disadvantajes
    + Requires to consider information asymmetry
    + The public can see more of what is reported than what is done
    + Opinion - can be used to do greenwashing
+ Behavioral Operations and Supply Chain Management (SCM)
  + Advantages:
    + Help to consider risk attitudes of decision makers (risk aversion and loss aversion traits)
    + Increase the importance of the trust level between buyers and suppliers
  + Disadvantages:
    + Still, nonrational mechanisms can affect decisions (personal biases and interpretation)
+ Behavioral Operations and Service Operations
  + Advantages
    + Optimize customer flow based on the human element
    + High industry applicability in grocery and others
  + Disadvantages
    + Non-rational behavior and information asymmetry can break down operations
+ Behavioral Operations and Process Improvement
  + Advantages
    + Improves knowledge sharing
    + Proposes technology as a cognitive aid
  + Disadvantages
    + Processes are often managed in linear paths
+ Machine learning for cyber defense
  + Advantage
    + Capability of choosing the best action to reduce the risk of compromise and attacker lateral movement
  + Disadvantage
    + Requires information from multiple sensors
    + Difficult to scale based on the number of states per node

#### Reflexion
+ Artificial intelligence has gained more importance over the years and shows critical advances in different fields. Still, one of the main issues is that the actions taken or suggested by AI are not always explainable, which can be used to avoid blame from decision makers. Without regulation and explainable AI, this can lead to dangerous decisions that could affect business and communities, particularly in security and risk management, where the main objective is to mitigate and reduce threats.
