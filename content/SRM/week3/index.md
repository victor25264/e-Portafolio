#### Introduction to Threat Modelling and Management
+ Popov, G. et al. (2022): "It is rare that a single method of risk assessment would adequately address every type of risk in a workplace. ... when selecting a risk assessment tool, the simplest method that provides sufficient information to make an appropriate risk management decision is advised." 


##### Threats and vulnerabilities
+ Vulnerability: weakness in a system, procedure, or control that a threat could exploit
+ Threat: event or circumstance with potential to damage the organization's operation, assets, or individuals.


##### Threat modeling
+ A form of risk assessment that models aspects of the attack and defense sides of a logical entity
+ Should be performed early in the development cycle to identify and remediate early 

###### Threat modeling manifesto
+ Culture of finding and fixing issues over checkbox compliance
+ People and collaboration over processes, methodology, and tools
+ A journey of understanding over a security or privacy snapshot
+ Doing threat modeling over talking about it
+ Continuous refinement over single delivery.

###### Thread modeling methodologies

+ Stride: (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of service, Elevation of privilege) closer to a more generic risk assessment process, but it's more technically based
+ PASTA (Process for Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis) is a risk-centric threat modeling framework. Consists of seven stages: define objectives, define technical scope, application decomposition, threat analysis, vulnerability and weakness analysis, attack modeling, and risk and impact analysis.
+ CVSS: The Common Vulnerability Scoring System is often referenced as a key metric for cybersecurity. 
+ Octave: Consists of three phases - build an asset-based threat profile, identify vulnerabilities, and develop a security strategy and plans. It has Octave-s for small organizations
+ Attack trees: built ot nalyzxe different security threats using different paths to achieve the attack goal
+ Trike
+ MITRE ATT&CK (Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge) is a framework for describing the actions an adversary may take while operating in an enterprise network.
+ OWASP (Open Web Application Security Project) is an organization that produces several resources helpful for threat modeling. It includes a Threat Modeling Cheat Sheet
+ Open Fair

#### Collaborative Discussion

##### Summary Post

New risks are being introduced with access to technology for the vulnerable population, as many of my peers agree with Amnon (2025), who raises the possibility that technology may overshadow the human element. I agree with this, and for these reasons, risk assessment is a valuable tool for improving these research areas. During these weeks, new topics have emerged that show promise for understanding and managing risk. While this theory is helpful, many authors do not follow it when introducing new technologies or processes to these groups. As with many other investigations, this is due to a lack of standard practice in doing risk assessments when presenting these solutions. This happens in cybersecurity, where many new solutions are proposed to address different problems, but few disclose the threat model used to develop them (Vesey 2025). In this case, when presenting processes and technologies to help vulnerable groups, a risk assessment should be conducted to ensure the proposed solutions are effective while minimizing potential risks. This will introduce more complexity when developing solutions, but it will also ensure that researchers introduce processes and tools that are the right fit for the job.



#### Reflexion
+ There are many methodologies for threat modeling with advantages and disadvantages. Having this many can be daunting for a new organization to start, when all these methodologies have formal definitions rather than layman's terms. On the other hand, as systems get more and more complex, risk management needs to include more and more variables, and some will be ignored because of a lack of understanding of the systems or because of complacency. Either way, software and asset inventory will be a critical part of risk management.
